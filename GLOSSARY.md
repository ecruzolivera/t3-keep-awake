# T3 Keep Awake

Local T3 activity monitoring and automatic sleep inhibition for the current Linux user.

## Language

**Activation**:
The transition from an installed companion to one that runs automatically and has confirmed connectivity to the selected local T3 environment.
_Avoid_: Started, as a synonym for successful activation

**Readiness**:
A running companion's confirmed ability to receive current activity from the selected local T3 environment.
_Avoid_: Running, as a synonym for readiness
